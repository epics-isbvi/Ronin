How to play

Taro carries his father's sword across Japan to learn the truth behind a name history called traitor. Talk to everyone, solve each land's trials, collect the key, and take the east gate onward. Your journal records what you learn, and the pause menu always shows your current objective.

    The Road Home

    Taro no longer needs the world to remember his father kindly. He knows what he chose, and why.
